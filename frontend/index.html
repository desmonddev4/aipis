Africa Infrastructure, Prosperity and Industrialisation Summit (AIPIS)

From financial close to prosperity close

The Africa Infrastructure, Prosperity and Industrialisation Summit (AIPIS) is an annual summit that measures African infrastructure by what it delivers: industrial output, jobs and better living standards.

Building infrastructure. Powering industry. Delivering prosperity.

Why AIPIS

Africa has made strong progress in raising finance for infrastructure. Governments, development finance institutions, pension funds and private investors now plan and fund projects with growing skill.
