About

Why AIPIS

Africa has made strong progress in raising finance for infrastructure. Governments, development finance institutions, pension funds and private investors now plan and fund projects with growing skill.

Problems remain after the funding is secured. Many projects stall, run over budget, or are completed without the industrial growth that justified them. AIPIS follows projects through construction and operation and measures what they deliver for industry and citizens.

Strategic theme, 2027 to 2031

Infrastructure that Delivers: Turning Africa’s Investment into Industry and Shared Prosperity

Every edition asks one question: has infrastructure spending in Africa produced working industries and better lives?

1

Financial close

The point at which a project’s funding is fully secured.

2

Prosperity close

The point at which a project can show measurable benefits to industry and citizens. AIPIS introduces this standard.

Innovations

The AIPIS Distinction: Seven Innovations

Each innovation builds on expertise the convenors already hold.

01

Prosperity Close Standard

A published standard that defines when a project has delivered measurable benefits: firms served, jobs sustained, local content achieved and reliable service.

02

Africa Infrastructure Prosperity Index

An annual ranking by KNUST and GCI of how well African countries turn infrastructure spending into industrial and social results.

03

Unfinished Projects Forensic Clinic

Led by AIPAF. Panels of engineers, quantity surveyors, lawyers and auditors examine stalled projects and set out the causes and a recovery plan.

04

Build-with-Africa Procurement Exchange

Pre-arranged meetings between project owners, contractors and African manufacturers of steel, cement, cables and building materials.

05

Site-to-Summit Field Day

A full day at live projects, industrial sites and local suppliers in the Ashanti Region.

06

Land, Traditional Authority and Infrastructure Roundtable

Traditional authorities, land agencies, developers and financiers discuss rights of way, compensation and benefit sharing.

07

Commitments Tracker and Kumasi Delivery Compact

Every pledge is recorded publicly and reported at the next edition. The summit closes with the signing of the Kumasi Delivery Compact.

Call for Speakers

Call for speakers, presenters and exhibitors

AIPIS 2027 invites nominations and applications for the roles below. Submission deadlines and requirements will be published with the full call. To express interest now, use the button under each role.

Distinguished Keynote Speakers

Heads of state and government, ministers, heads of development finance institutions and other leaders of international standing. They open each conference day and set out the direction on infrastructure, industry and prosperity in Africa. Distinguished Keynote Speakers are invited by the Summit Steering Committee; nominations are welcome.

Special Guest Speakers

Senior figures from government, traditional authority, industry, finance and civil society. They speak at plenaries, roundtables and the Finance and Investor Forum, sharing first-hand experience of planning, funding and delivering infrastructure.

Technical Keynote Presenters

Leading researchers and practitioners who present evidence and lessons on project delivery, assurance and forensics, energy, industrial policy and sustainability. Their presentations anchor the peer-reviewed research and practice stream and are published in the summit proceedings.

Poster Presenters

Researchers, postgraduate students and young professionals who present research, case studies or project results in the poster exhibition. Posters are on display throughout the summit and give delegates a direct way to discuss new work with the authors.

Exhibitors

Contractors, manufacturers, consultants, public agencies, universities and technology providers who show their products, services and projects in the exhibition area. An exhibition booth of 6 sq m costs USD 5,000 and includes 2 delegate passes and a listing in the exhibitor directory. Thirty booths are available.

Who attends

Who will be in the room

AIPIS brings together the people who finance, build, operate and audit infrastructure, and the communities it serves.

Governments and public agencies

Ministers and officials responsible for roads, energy, works and housing, trade and industry, finance and project delivery.

Financiers

Development finance institutions, commercial banks, pension and insurance funds, sovereign wealth funds and infrastructure fund managers.

Industry

Universities and young professionals
