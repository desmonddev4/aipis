Innovations

The AIPIS Distinction: Seven Innovations

Each innovation builds on expertise the convenors already hold.

01

Prosperity Close Standard

A published standard that defines when a project has delivered measurable benefits: firms served, jobs sustained, local content achieved and reliable service.

02

Africa Infrastructure Prosperity Index

An annual ranking by KNUST and GCI of how well African countries turn infrastructure spending into industrial and social results.

03

Unfinished Projects Forensic Clinic

Led by AIPAF. Panels of engineers, quantity surveyors, lawyers and auditors examine stalled projects and set out the causes and a recovery plan.

04

Build-with-Africa Procurement Exchange

Pre-arranged meetings between project owners, contractors and African manufacturers of steel, cement, cables and building materials.

05

Site-to-Summit Field Day

A full day at live projects, industrial sites and local suppliers in the Ashanti Region.

06

Land, Traditional Authority and Infrastructure Roundtable

Traditional authorities, land agencies, developers and financiers discuss rights of way, compensation and benefit sharing.

07

Commitments Tracker and Kumasi Delivery Compact

Every pledge is recorded publicly and reported at the next edition. The summit closes with the signing of the Kumasi Delivery Compact.
