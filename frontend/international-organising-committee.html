International Organising Committee

Meet the international team shaping the AIPIS programme, building continental partnerships and connecting the summit to leaders across Africa and the diaspora.

Governance

Continental reach, practical delivery

The International Organising Committee gives AIPIS its continental reach. It shapes the edition theme and programme, identifies speakers and partners, promotes the research and project streams, and works with the Local Organising Committee to deliver the summit.

Programme

Sets session topics and connects the programme to the summit’s strategic theme.

Partnerships

Builds links with universities, governments, financiers and industry across Africa.

Delivery

Oversees the Prosperity Index, forensic report and commitments tracker for each edition.
