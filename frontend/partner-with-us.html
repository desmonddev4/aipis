Partner with AIPIS

Partners are recognised at the summit and in its published outputs, which remain in use after each edition. Fees are in US dollars and payable in Ghana cedis at the Bank of Ghana rate on the date of payment. Founding Partners hold first right of renewal for the 2028 to 2031 editions.
