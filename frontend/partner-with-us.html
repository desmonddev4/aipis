Call for partners and sponsors

Partners are recognised at the summit and in its published outputs, which remain in use after each edition. Fees are in US dollars and payable in Ghana cedis at the Bank of Ghana rate on the date of payment. Founding Partners hold first right of renewal for the 2028 to 2031 editions.

Why partner

  • Reach ministers, financiers, contractors and manufacturers from across Africa in one place
  • Associate your organisation with the Prosperity Index, the Forensic Clinic and the AIPIS Delivery Compact
  • Meet project owners and buyers at the Build-with-Africa Procurement Exchange
  • Founding Partners hold first right of renewal for the 2028 to 2031 editions

How to partner

  1. Request the partnership prospectus from the AIPIS Secretariat
  2. Choose a package, or ask for one shaped around your objectives
  3. Complete the Partnership Commitment Form
  4. Receive your partnership agreement and invoice

Bookings received by 31 January 2027 receive full recognition in all summit materials.
