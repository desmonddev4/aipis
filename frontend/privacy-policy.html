Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

AIPIS is committed to protecting the privacy and security of the information shared with us through our website, event registration forms, invitation requests, partner enquiries, and public communications.

1. Information we collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, organisation, email address, phone number, country, role, and any details submitted through registration, speaker, roundtable invitation, partnership, or contact forms. We may also collect technical information such as browser type, device information, referring pages, and usage data to improve the experience of our website.

2. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Process registrations, speaking submissions, roundtable invitation requests, partnership enquiries, and general event enquiries.
  • Communicate event updates, confirmations, schedules, and relevant programme information.
  • Respond to questions and provide support to delegates, partners, speakers, and exhibitors.
  • Improve the security, functionality, and performance of our site and communications.
  • Meet legal, operational, and reporting obligations where required.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who support our website, event administration, communications, or data processing activities, and only where necessary to fulfil the purpose for which the information was provided. We may also disclose information where required by law, professional obligations, or to protect the rights and safety of participants.

4. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain records required for legitimate business operations.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data that you have provided to us, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. If you would like to exercise these rights or ask a question about your personal information, please contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact the AIPIS secretariat at info@aipisgh.org or call +233 (0) 243 555 882.
