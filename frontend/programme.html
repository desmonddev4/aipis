Programme

Four days in Kumasi

The 2027 theme is From Financial Close to Prosperity Close: Delivering Infrastructure that Builds African Industry. Each conference day covers one thematic area, and the fourth day is spent in the field.

Day 1

Tuesday 24 August

Building infrastructure

Opening ceremony and launch of the Africa Infrastructure Prosperity Index; Unfinished Projects Forensic Clinic; Land, Traditional Authority and Infrastructure Roundtable.

Day 2

Wednesday 25 August

Powering industry

Plenary on energy for industry; Build-with-Africa Procurement Exchange; transport corridors, special economic zones and industrial parks; round-the-clock production under the 24-Hour Economy.

Day 3

Thursday 26 August

Delivering prosperity

Prosperity Close Standard; Finance and Investor Forum; research and practice papers; signing of the Kumasi Delivery Compact and closing ceremony.

Day 4

Friday 27 August

Site-to-Summit Field Day

Visits to live projects, industrial sites and local manufacturers in the Ashanti Region.

Four topics run through every session: Project assuranceGreen deliverySkillsLand and community
