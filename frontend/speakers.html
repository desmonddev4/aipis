Call for Speakers

Call for speakers, presenters and exhibitors

AIPIS 2027 invites nominations and applications for the roles below. Submission deadlines and requirements will be published with the full call. To express interest now, use the button under each role.

Distinguished Keynote Speakers

Heads of state and government, ministers, heads of development finance institutions and other leaders of international standing. They open each conference day and set out the direction on infrastructure, industry and prosperity in Africa. Distinguished Keynote Speakers are invited by the Summit Steering Committee; nominations are welcome.

Special Guest Speakers

Senior figures from government, traditional authority, industry, finance and civil society. They speak at plenaries, roundtables and the Finance and Investor Forum, sharing first-hand experience of planning, funding and delivering infrastructure.

Technical Keynote Presenters

Leading researchers and practitioners who present evidence and lessons on project delivery, assurance and forensics, energy, industrial policy and sustainability. Their presentations anchor the peer-reviewed research and practice stream and are published in the summit proceedings.

Poster Presenters

Researchers, postgraduate students and young professionals who present research, case studies or project results in the poster exhibition. Posters are on display throughout the summit and give delegates a direct way to discuss new work with the authors.

Exhibitors

Contractors, manufacturers, consultants, public agencies, universities and technology providers who show their products, services and projects in the exhibition area. An exhibition booth of 6 sq m costs USD 5,000 and includes 2 delegate passes and a listing in the exhibitor directory. Thirty booths are available.
